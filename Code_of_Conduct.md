# Code of Conduct

## Guiding Principle

This project, as well as any derivative works, is conceived as a tool in service of the common good. Its use and development must at all times be oriented toward the progress of humanity, respect for all forms of life, and the strengthening of the ecosystems and communities that sustain our world.

## Fundamental Commitments

Those who use, modify, or distribute this project commit to ensuring that its application:

- Promotes social welfare, equity, and distributive justice.
- Respects human, labor, and collective rights, in line with international standards.
- Favors environmental sustainability and the responsible use of natural resources.

## Prohibited Uses

This project, or any derivative thereof, **MAY NOT** be used for purposes that undermine human dignity, peace, or ecological balance. In particular, it may not be employed for:

- Automated decision-making that affects legal or fundamental rights without meaningful human review, appeal, or transparency.
- Armed conflicts, warfare, genocide, crimes against humanity, or activities that impair the self-determination of peoples. This includes the development or enhancement of autonomous weapons systems, lethal or non-lethal, that operate without meaningful human control or regulation.
- The design, development, or implementation of systems that perpetuate or increase socioeconomic inequality.
- Practices that violate workers' rights, including forced labor, child exploitation, or any form of modern slavery. This includes the automation or augmentation of tasks that result in large-scale workforce displacement without accompanying measures for retraining, income protection, or social dialogue, particularly when such displacement affects already marginalized communities or regions with limited economic alternatives.
- Activities involving the irresponsible extraction, use, or degradation of natural resources, with special attention to those affecting fragile ecosystems or communities dependent on them. This includes any form of use in climate-critical infrastructure (energy grids, water management, transportation systems) without documented environmental impact assessment and fail-safe mechanisms that prioritize ecological and public safety over operational efficiency.
- Electoral campaigns, partisan political propaganda, or any activity intended to influence electoral processes in a non-transparent or manipulative manner.
- Generation, amplification, or dissemination of synthetic media (deepfakes, voice cloning, etc.) intended to deceive, defraud, or manipulate public opinion, unless clearly labeled as synthetic and used for legitimate artistic, educational, or journalistic purposes.
- Mass surveillance systems, non-consensual population control, or any other form of intrusion into privacy and individual liberties. This includes activities that systematically extract, commodify, or monetize personal or community data without free, prior, and informed consent, including but not limited to behavioral profiling, emotional recognition, or biometric identification in public spaces. It also includes the implementation of social credit systems, citizen scoring, or any reputation-based access mechanism that conditions fundamental rights (healthcare, education, mobility, employment, political participation, etc) on algorithmic evaluations of behavior, loyalty, or social compliance.
- Deployment of algorithmic systems that produce systematically disparate outcomes across demographic groups (race, gender, socioeconomic status, disability, age, etc.) without independent, periodic, and publicly accessible fairness audits, and without corrective mechanisms for identified biases.

This list is not exhaustive. Any use that, by its nature or consequences, contravenes the guiding principle of this code shall be considered equally prohibited, even if not explicitly enumerated.

## Scope and Responsibility

This code of conduct is binding upon every individual or organization that makes use of the project, whether for commercial, academic, governmental, or private purposes.

Any organization or individual using this project shall maintain a publicly accessible record of its deployment contexts, impact assessments, and any reported violations. Failure to do so constitutes a presumption of non-compliance.

## Interpretation and Evolution

This code shall be interpreted broadly and progressively, adhering to the spirit of its principles rather than their strict letter. The community is encouraged to propose improvements and updates that reflect emerging ethical challenges, ensuring that the project remains aligned with the values that inspire it.

This code applies regardless of the jurisdiction in which the project is used. No national law, local regulation, or contractual obligation may be invoked to justify a violation of these provisions. In case of conflict, the most protective interpretation for human rights and ecological integrity shall prevail.
